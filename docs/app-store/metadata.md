## Promotional text

## Description

JobSwipe turns an overwhelming job market into a personal, decision-ready feed.

Set the roles, salary floor, seniority, location, work style, eligibility, and exclusions that matter to you. JobSwipe qualifies listings before ranking them, so obvious conflicts stay out of the deck.

Use Matches for direct-fit roles that clear every hard rule. Use Stretch for plausible adjacent opportunities that need your judgment. Swipe to pass or save, open any card for the complete listing and company context, and organize saved roles with application deadlines, follow-ups, contacts, and notes.

JobSwipe also learns from optional pass reasons while keeping your hard filters under your control. Fit labels compare available listing evidence with your profile; they are not promises of an interview or offer.

Key features:

- Direct Matches and clearly explained Stretch roles
- Salary, seniority, location, work-mode, and eligibility filters
- Complete employer listing and company context
- Saved-job search, filters, notes, and application planning
- Resume, skill, experience, education, and portfolio evidence
- Same-workspace device pairing
- Data export and full workspace deletion
- Accessible buttons and keyboard controls in addition to swipe gestures

## Review notes draft

JobSwipe does not require account creation. On first launch, complete the guided setup. The app stores a private workspace using a signed installation session. To test the core experience:

1. Complete setup with at least one role, salary floor, work mode, and seniority.
2. Open Matches for direct roles or Stretch for adjacent roles.
3. Swipe right to save or left to pass. Buttons and keyboard alternatives are also available in job details.
4. Open Saved to search, filter, and add application details.
5. Open You → Privacy & data to export or permanently delete the workspace.

Employer application links open the original employer page. No purchase is required. If the review catalog is empty, the submission build must include a seeded review workspace or App Review demo instructions with live sample listings.
